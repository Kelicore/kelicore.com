Recent work

Pavilions Teignmouth

Pavilions Teignmouth had struggled for years with poor internet connectivity and network reliability issues. Kelicore carried out a full audit and assessment, then installed a new leased line with a failover fibre-to-the-cabinet (FTTC) connection, put in a new phone system, and made significant network management changes to improve performance and security. The result is a faster, more secure and more reliable service, with 100% uptime since Kelicore took over the network.
