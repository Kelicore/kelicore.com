Proof

Pavilions Teignmouth

Pavilions Teignmouth had struggled for years with poor internet connectivity and network reliability issues. Kelicore carried out a full audit and assessment, then installed a new leased line with a failover fibre-to-the-cabinet (FTTC) connection, put in a new phone system, and made significant network management changes to improve performance and security. The result is a better performing, more secure and more reliable service, with 100% uptime since Kelicore took over the network. It is exactly the kind of dependable, behind-the-scenes work we are built for.

“It is like a weight has been lifted. The service is reliable, and Ben and the team are always happy to help.”

Nikki, Manager, Pavilions Teignmouth

About Kelicore

Kelicore is a founder-led infrastructure practice, built on around 20 years of hands-on experience in technology, including 14 years building large-scale WiFi networks for venues across the UK: hundreds of venues, thousands of concurrent users, in spaces where failure on the night is not an option.

That background spans cloud and security engineering, on-premises systems, and the kind of high-density, demanding WiFi environments most providers never encounter.

Senior-led, hands-on, and based in Devon. You always deal directly with the founder, with trusted engineering support brought in for larger jobs. Every engagement is run at the same level of attention, whether it is a venue network, a cloud platform, or a server room.
